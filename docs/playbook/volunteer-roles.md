# Volunteer roles (template)

Each role matches a value of **Role** on Volunteer Shift.

| Role | What they do | Typical shift |
|---|---|---|
| Registration Desk | Check attendees in, hand out badges, answer questions | Early morning to late morning |
| Room Host | Keep sessions on time, help speakers set up, count attendance | Half day per room |
| Speaker Liaison | Greet speakers, take them to their room, look after their needs | Full day |
| Social Media | Post photos and highlights through the day | Full day, can rotate |
| Setup and Teardown | Signage, stage setup and pack-down | Before opening and after closing |

**Tips:** brief every volunteer before doors open, give each one a named contact on the organizing team, and thank them publicly after the event.
