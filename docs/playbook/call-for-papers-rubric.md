# Call for papers review rubric (template)

Each reviewer scores a submission from 1 to 5 in a **Submission Review** record. A submission needs at least one review before it can be accepted.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Must have. Clear takeaways, strong fit, well-matched speaker |
| 4 | Strong. Minor gaps in focus or fit |
| 3 | Solid but not distinctive. Accept if the track needs it |
| 2 | Weak fit or unclear takeaways |
| 1 | Not a fit for this event |

**What to look for:**
- Will attendees leave able to do something new?
- Does the level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) match the abstract?
- Does it fill a gap in its track?
- Is the session sales-free?

**Balancing the agenda:** after scoring, sort by Average Score within each track. Then check the overall mix of levels, formats and speakers, including first-time speakers, before you finalize.
