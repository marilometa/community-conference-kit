# Planning timeline (template)

A starting point for a one-day community conference. Adjust to your event's size.

| When | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 12 months out | Set the date, confirm the venue, form the organizing team |
| 10 months out | Publish the sponsor prospectus and start sponsor outreach |
| 8 months out | Open the call for papers. Recruit reviewers |
| 6 months out | Close the call for papers. Review and score every submission |
| 5 months out | Notify speakers. Draft the agenda by track and room |
| 4 months out | Publish the agenda. Open registration |
| 2 months out | Recruit volunteers and assign shifts |
| 2 weeks out | Final run-of-show, speaker briefing, sponsor deliverables check |
| Event day | Registration desk, room hosts, speaker liaison, social media |
| 1 week after | Thank speakers, sponsors and volunteers. Share recordings and feedback |

In Salesforce, move the Conference **Status** through Planning, Call for Papers Open, Agenda Published, Live and Completed as you go.
